Analog Inspiration

Two cards for this month

DRAFT, Jason to rewrite: two moves from the Analog Inspiration deck, one for teachers and one to hand to students.

AuthenticityTeacher move

Ask for assessments that look like real work

Give AI your learning outcomes and ask for three different authentic assessment ideas built on them. Tell it the assessments should emphasize real world application and mirror the tasks, scenarios, workflows and deliverables students will actually run into later, at work and in life.

DRAFT, Jason to rewrite: take one unit you already teach, paste in the standards, and see if any of the three is better than the test you have been giving.
BiasStudent move

Put students to work as AI auditors

Have students dig into how AI tools get things wrong, then test the tools themselves to surface errors, inconsistencies and buried assumptions in their own subject. What words does it use in this field? Whose perspective is missing from the answer? What does an image generator assume when you ask it for a picture? Then have them share what they found with the class.

DRAFT, Jason to rewrite: works in any subject, and students are usually faster at breaking these tools than we are.
